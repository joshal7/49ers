## Core Engineering Philosophy: Quality vs. Speed Decision Matrix

When building or modifying features in this repository, adhere strictly to the two-tier performance philosophy (see `ARCHITECTURE.md` for full details):

1. **When to Push for Accuracy, Depth & Audio Realism Over Generation Time (`accuracy_over_latency`)**:
   - **Applies to**: On-demand AI Deep Analysis (`/api/analyze`), On-demand Autopodcast Script & Audio Generation (`/api/podcast`), Scheduled Daily Briefs (`scripts/lib/aiSynthesisEngine.mjs`), and Studio `.m4a` Podcast Builds (`scripts/build-podcasts.mjs`).
   - **Rule**: Never rush, truncate verification, or approximate CBA contract math. Use the highest-scoring free-tier Gemini model (`gemini-4-hydrogen` via `scoreFreeTierGeminiModel` / `scoreBestGeminiReasoningModel`, cascading to `gemini-3.8-flash` `HIGH`): keep maximum **`XHIGH`** thinking (`thinkingLevel: "XHIGH"`) on non-time-sensitive daily/scheduled syntheses (`1/day 5:00 PM PT Top-News Brief`, `1/day Matchup Intel`, and `scripts/lib/aiSynthesisEngine.mjs`), and use **`HIGH`** thinking (`thinkingLevel: "HIGH"`) on live user-waiting processing (`Auto Pod`, `On-Demand AI Deep Analysis`, and live quota probes) for ~35–50% faster turnaround over pre-compiled Stage-1 OverTheCap/EPA dossiers + live Google Search Grounding without sacrificing analytical or conversational quality.
   - **Audio Studio Quality**: Cascade `gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts` → `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts` on the Edge Worker (or `edge-tts` Multilingual Neural voices + 44.1 kHz 7-stage broadcast DSP mastering in `scripts/build-podcasts.mjs`), always passing dialogue through `humanizeNflTextForTts` first.
   - **UX Wait Transparency, Streamlined Controls & Group Deduplication**: Always show concise pre-click rough duration hints (`[data-est-wait-preview]`, without verbose internal model-version clutter) and the live active job banner (`[data-job-progress="active"]` with spinner, 4-bar audio wave, progress bar, elapsed clock, and `Accuracy-First` indicator). Keep podcast `Host Format` and `Default Podcast Length` inside top-right `Settings`. Cache completed outputs in Cloudflare KV (`SHARED_CACHE`) and R2 (`PODCAST_BUCKET`) so the other ~24 friends get `<50ms` instant reads.

2. **When to Push for Sub-Second Interactivity & Sub-Minute Live Telemetry**:
   - **Applies to**: App boot & offline shell (`sw.js`, `<300ms`, `2.2s` warm-cache timeout), lazy per-tab rendering (`src/app.js`), health wire reconciliation (`<35ms`), 5-year Armchair GM cap simulation (`<5ms`), 10,000-run Empirical-Bayes Playoff Monte Carlo rooting toggles (`<150ms`), 3-Lens Big Board queries (`<5ms`), roster search/sort, jargon popovers, and in-game injury wire polling.
   - **Rule**: Quantitative engines in `src/engine/` must remain 100% deterministic and synchronous in-browser. Live client telemetry in `src/services/dataService.js` polls 7 live channels (ESPN 24/7 X-Beat Player Wire, RotoWire NFL RSS, Official 49ers/ESPN Transactions, ESPN Rapid NFL Breaking Desk, Official `49ers.com` News & Video RSS, Bluesky AT Protocol, and optional Edge Worker `/api/live-wire`, all bounded by `6–8.5s` `AbortController` timeouts) every **20 seconds during active 49ers game windows** (`is49ersGameLiveWindow`) and every **45 seconds** otherwise (plus `60s` per-team `~275 KB` ESPN `injuries?team=` & live score/postseason overlay), elevating live in-game updates (`LIVE INJURY UPDATE`) to `#1` while balancing top salience (`balanceFeedTopSalience`) for credible trade rumors, legal news, and player interviews.
